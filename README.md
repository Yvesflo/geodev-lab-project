# Flood exposure of health facilities along the Logone river, Mayo-Danay, Far North Cameroon

A GeoDev Lab Africa project, built over twelve months.

## Question

Which health facilities in the Kaï-Kaï, Vélé and Yagoua health districts were flooded or cut off during the 2024 and 2025 flood seasons, how many children under five lost walking access to care as a result, and did this change between the two years?

## Approach

I map flood extent from Sentinel-1 radar imagery in Google Earth Engine. Each flood season is compared with a multi-year radar reference for the same weeks, so that the normal seasonal inundation of the Logone floodplain is not counted as flooding. The 2024 map is validated against the Copernicus Emergency Management Service products for that event.

The flood maps are then overlaid on the 2024 national census of health facilities and on WorldPop estimates of the under-five population. In the last step, AccessMod compares walking travel time to the nearest health facility in the dry season and during the flood.
