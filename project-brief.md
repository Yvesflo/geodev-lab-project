# My Project Brief

## The question
Which schools in the Far North Region, Cameroon, are at flood risk during the last two years

## The data I need

- Monthly rainfall, 24 months - CHIRPS - https://data.chc.ucsb.edu/products/CHIRPS-2.0/africa_monthly/tifs/.chc.ucsb.edu/products/CHIRPS-2.0/) 
- Administrative Boundaries - GADM - https://gadm.org/download_country.html
- School locations - OSM via QuickOSM (QGIS) extracted from Far North Region Boundaries
- Elevation (DEM) - Copernicus GLO-30 via OpenTopography - https://portal.opentopography.org - GeoTIFF, 30 m
- Watercourses/rivers - OpenStreetMap, via QuickOSM (QGIS).
- Land cover - ESA WorldCover 10 m - https://esa-worldcover.org - GeoTIFF
- Population / school-age population - https://hub.worldpop.org - GeoTIFF, ~100 m
